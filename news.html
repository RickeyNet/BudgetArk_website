September 2, 2026 Release v1.10.1

Banks that keep up, plans that add up, and lump sum vs. monthly

Two halves to this one. On the bank side, a connected account can now land in any Bridge category - 401k, investment, HSA, not just checking - and the app guesses the right one from the bank's own account name. If a bank on your SimpleFIN bridge needs a fresh login, BudgetArk now says so instead of quietly reporting "last synced 2 hours ago", and when the bank comes back it re-imports the gap on its own (up to 90 days). Two phones on the same bank no longer ask you to review the same transactions twice: approvals and skips travel with partner sync. Also fixed: the wizard's Connected screen could freeze the app on Done, and the setup screen's take-home pay and rent fields no longer hide behind the keyboard.

On the planning side, Purchase Plans add up: a summary of everything saved and still to go, snowball-style ordering, one monthly amount split across plans or poured into one at a time, a stacked savings chart, cost per use, what-if chips, and a line showing what the same money would do on your highest-rate debt. The Review Inbox can send a transfer straight to a plan's balance, offers "Always approve as…" after three matching approvals, and flags unusually large charges. The Bridge shows what a savings account earns at its APY, a new Subscription Detective finds recurring charges with no bill on file, and People gains Settle Up for monthly shared expenses. And the Compound Interest Calculator can now compare a lump sum invested once against monthly contributions - what each becomes, what both become together, and the year the monthly plan overtakes the lump sum. The same comparison is live in the investment growth calculator on this site.

August 28, 2026 Release v1.10.0

Bills that settle themselves, cards that track themselves, and sturdier sync

Bills that change every month now settle themselves. Set electric or water up once with your best estimate; when the real charge arrives - from your bank or typed in with the new "Log actual" button - file it against the bill and the actual replaces the estimate everywhere, with no double counting. Tick "Always do this" and next month's charge is filed automatically; when actuals drift, editing the bill offers the three-month average in one tap. The Review Inbox can even spot a merchant that has charged monthly for three months and offer to create the bill for you. Credit cards on the Debts tab can now follow the connected bank account behind them, so the balance updates after every sync - with a switch to turn mirroring off, and the payoff ring never going negative when you charge more than the original balance.

The Budget tab learned a lot: a Limits sheet that sets every category at once ("Copy last month", "Use 3-month averages"), pace marks on every limited category that turn amber when you're spending faster than the month allows, a Spending Pace card that says so plainly, and real dates on entries instead of everything landing on the 15th. The Add Entry form remembers descriptions you've used, switches category when it recognises a name, and has "Save + another"; a "+ New" pill creates a category on the spot, built-in categories can be hidden, and an expense can be split across several people with per-person totals that still add up. The Bridge gains a "This month" card with your spending pace, last three entries, and a quick add. New installs open with the mission statement, can start from a quick-start budget template (single, couple, debt-heavy, zero-based) sized to your pay and rent, and are asked about Tracking Reminders during setup. After the occasional win, a small Tip Jar note explains that tips are optional and unlock nothing.

Under the hood, a deep pass over storage, sync, and restore. Fixed: a save on the Budget or Bridge tab could undo what a partner or bank sync had just written; removed budget limits never reached a partner's phone; restoring a backup now tells the next sync to start from scratch; and on Android, exporting a backup over about 1 MB could stall the app - backups are now shared as files with no size limit. Bank transactions match their posted versions even when the bank rewrites the description, spreadsheet exports carry keep-alive settings, applied payment amounts, and fund holdings, lesson progress rides along in backups, the app asks before the first exchange-rate fetch, storage errors now show a message where they happen, and plaintext export files are deleted the moment the share sheet closes. About 600 new automated checks cover storage, sync, bank import, and every calculator. This one is a store update on both platforms.

August 26, 2026 Release v1.9.3

Change your bank account choices anytime

The account mapping you picked when you first connected a bank was one-shot - not anymore. Every linked account in the Connections manager now has its own "Import transactions" switch and "Balance updates" picker, so you can map an account to the Bridge (or create a new one inline) whenever you like. Also fixed: pending bank transactions that occasionally resurfaced as duplicates once they posted, and the Tip Jar can now log a completed tip as a Giving entry.

August 20, 2026 Release v1.9.2

Emergency fund, linked to your real savings

Mark one or more savings accounts on the Bridge as your emergency fund and BudgetArk reads the fund's value straight from those balances - kept current by bank-connection updates. The Keel and Deck milestones in Build Your Ark, the Galley Stocked badge, the Budget tab, and the Charts emergency-fund plan all show the same number. Backups and partner sync carry the designation too.

August 14, 2026 Release v1.9.1

Stability & security fixes - and Android enters closed testing

Fixes an onboarding freeze on some iPhones, storage failures at startup that could re-run onboarding or blank the Profile tab, and patches dependency security advisories. This is also the first build in the Google Play closed test - the public Android launch is getting closer.

July 27, 2026 Update

Six more themes - sixteen in all

Slate pairs graphite greys with a mustard accent for a calm, focused look. Classic is a straight-faced throwback to the silver-and-teal desktops of 1998, square corners and all. Lighthouse is a true high-contrast theme - every color audited to at least 7:1 for maximum readability. Chart Room is a nautical chart with land-tone cards on sea blue, Harbor Dawn a peach sunrise over seafoam cards, and Ledger the classic green-bar accounting paper. Try every one of them on the themes section of this site.

July 27, 2026 Update

App Lock, automatic backups, and a safe-to-spend number

Turn on App Lock and BudgetArk asks for a PIN whenever it opens - the PIN never leaves your device. Scheduled local auto-backups (weekly or monthly, encrypted with your device key) protect people who never tap Export, and password-protected exports are now tamper-evident. On the Budget tab, enter what's in checking at the start of the month and get a projected month-end and a real safe-to-spend figure. Also new: a US take-home pay calculator with federal, state, and FICA tables that never phone home; Private entries that stay off your partner's device; a merchant-rules manager for changing "always do this" decisions; and the Quick Entry widget arrives on iOS.

July 20, 2026 Update

Search everything, count down to debt-free, and new Charts tools

Global search with advanced filters finds any debt, payment, or budget entry from the Debts and Budget tabs. The Debts tab gains a Debt-Free Countdown built from your real payment pace, plus a credit-card keep-alive watch that warns you before an unused card gets closed. The Charts tab picks up a currency exchange calculator, a "What if I stopped spending on…" projection, and a Plan-a-Purchase sinking-fund tool that tracks on the Bridge - where every account and category now shows how much it's up or down over the window you pick. Onboarding is now one searchable guide with a feature tour you can replay anytime.

July 11, 2026 Release v1.9.0

Bank Connections - your bank, on your terms

Bring your own SimpleFIN or Teller credentials and let transactions import themselves. Nothing enters your budget until you approve it in the new Review Inbox, credentials stay encrypted on your device, and BudgetArk still operates no server - it never sits between you and your bank. The same release adds encrypted receipt photos and business expense tracking with a tax-time report, W-2 / 1099 paycheck tagging, assigning spending to people in your household, opt-in tracking reminders that never put amounts on your lock screen, an Android Quick Entry home-screen widget, the Deep Sea ambient theme, and an optional Tip Jar that unlocks nothing because everything is already free.

July 7, 2026 Release v1.8.3

Captain's Course is complete

All 24 lessons across five chapters are now aboard: Stocking the Galley (emergency funds, HYSAs, sinking funds), Catching Wind (compounding, index funds, 401(k)/IRA/Roth, asset allocation), and Charting Far Waters (net worth, buy vs. rent, insurance, estate basics) join the first two chapters. The Budget tab's Debt Payments section also got a correctness pass - no more phantom planned rows in past months.

June 27, 2026 Release v1.8.0

Live stock & crypto holdings on the Bridge

Add tickers under each brokerage on the Bridge and see their market value roll into your net worth. The feature is opt-in and shows a plain-language disclosure first: only ticker symbols leave your phone - never share counts, balances, or who you are - through a private proxy that stores no portfolio data. Crypto pairs like BTC/USD work too, converted into your display currency. Prices update daily as of v1.8.1.

June 19, 2026 Release v1.7.4

Swedish Krona and real currency conversion

SEK joins the currency list, and switching currencies now offers to convert every stored amount at today's rate - or just change the symbol if your numbers are already in the new currency. Default milestone targets are localized for new plans, and achievement progress reads in your own currency.

June 9, 2026 Release v1.7.2

Reliability + sync security

Partner sync moves to protocol v2 with the whole message envelope authenticated (both phones need this version to sync again). Net-worth history and custom categories now sync between paired devices, deleted items stay deleted after a sync, backups grew much larger import limits, and a dozen date and timezone edge cases were fixed across budgets and debt payments.

Debt due-date reminders + Captain's Course Chapter 2

Never miss a payment again: every debt can now carry its own payment due day, and a reminder banner on the Budget and Debts tabs shows what's coming up - with a one-tap prompt to log the payment when the day arrives.
