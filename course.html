CAPTAIN'S COURSE

Learn the seas. Plot your course.

The full money course from the app's Charts tab: 24 short lessons in 5 chapters, about 2 hours in all. Free to read, no signup, nothing tracked.

CHAPTER 1 · BUDGETING BASICS

⛵ Setting Sail

5 lessons · 22 min

  1. 🧭 What a budget really is A budget is not a restriction. It is a plan you write before the month spends you. 3 min
  2. ⚖️ Needs, Wants, Savings (50/30/20) The simplest budget framework in the world. Three buckets, three percentages. 4 min
  3. 🗒️ Tracking vs. budgeting Two different jobs. Most apps blur them. The blur is why most budgets feel pointless. 5 min
  4. 0️⃣ Zero-based budgeting Every dollar gets a job before the month starts. Income minus assignments equals zero. 6 min
  5. 🔭 Reading your monthly review The review is the cheapest financial coach you'll ever have. Most people skip it. 4 min

CHAPTER 2 · DEBT

🔨 Patching the Hull

6 lessons · 32 min

  1. ⚓ Good debt vs bad debt Textbooks split debt into good and bad. We treat almost all consumer debt, including car loans, as bad debt worth paying down. 4 min
  2. 📈 How interest actually works Interest is rent on borrowed money. The APR tells you how expensive that rent is per year. 6 min
  3. 🛡️ The $1,000 starter emergency fund Before you throw every dollar at debt, keep a small cushion so the next surprise does not become new debt. 5 min
  4. ❄️ Snowball vs Avalanche Two valid payoff orders. Avalanche saves the most interest. Snowball wins the most momentum. 7 min
  5. 🔄 Refinancing - when it pays Refinancing swaps your old loan for a new one. It only helps when the math beats the fees. 6 min
  6. 💸 The debt snowflake (windfalls) Snowball and avalanche are the rivers. Snowflakes are the one-time bursts that speed the melt. 4 min

CHAPTER 3 · SAVING

🍞 Stocking the Galley

4 lessons · 19 min

  1. 🛟 Emergency fund - 3 to 6 months The starter fund keeps a bad week off your card. The full fund keeps a bad year from sinking the ship. 5 min
  2. 🏦 High-yield savings 101 Same insurance, same access, ten to twenty times the interest. Moving your savings is an hour of work you do once. 4 min
  3. 🪣 Sinking funds Most 'emergencies' are actually predictable. A sinking fund pays for December in July, one small deposit at a time. 5 min
  4. ⚓ Where to park short-term cash Money you need within five years doesn't belong in the market. Match where the cash sits to when you'll need it. 5 min

CHAPTER 4 · INVESTING

📈 Catching Wind

5 lessons · 28 min

  1. 🌱 Why compounding is the whole game Money earns returns, then the returns earn returns. Given enough years, the snowball dwarfs the snow you threw on it. 5 min
  2. 🧺 Index funds in one page Instead of guessing which companies win, buy a tiny slice of all of them for nearly free. Boring, and it beats most professionals. 5 min
  3. 🪜 401(k), IRA, Roth - which first? These aren't investments - they're tax shelters you put investments inside. Fill them in the right order and the same dollars retire richer. 7 min
  4. ⚖️ Asset allocation by decade How much stocks versus bonds matters more than which fund you pick. The right mix is the riskiest one you can hold without selling in a panic. 6 min
  5. 🧯 Avoiding the big mistakes Investing success is mostly subtraction. Avoid a handful of classic errors and the boring plan does the rest. 5 min

CHAPTER 5 · WEALTH & BEYOND

🗺️ Charting Far Waters

4 lessons · 21 min

  1. 🧭 Net worth - the only score Everything you own minus everything you owe. One number that tells the truth when income, spending, and appearances all lie. 4 min
  2. 🏠 Buy vs rent the math Renting is not throwing money away, and buying is not automatically winning. The honest comparison has more line items than the mortgage. 6 min
  3. ☂️ Insurance - what you actually need Insure the catastrophes you couldn't absorb, self-insure the annoyances you could, and skip the products that are mostly commission. 6 min
  4. 📜 Estate basics (will, beneficiaries, TOD) A few forms decide whether the wealth you built lands with your people or in a yearlong court process. Most of them are free. 5 min
