WHAT'S ON BOARD

Everything you need for the voyage

Debt payoff, budgeting, bank syncing, net worth, and planning for the future - all in one vessel.

  • ⚓

    Debt Tracker

    Crush every debt with Snowball, Avalanche, or your own payoff order. Due-date reminders, a Debt-Free Countdown built from your real payment pace, a keep-alive watch so a quiet credit card never gets closed on you, and cards that mirror their connected bank account so the balance updates after every sync.

  • 💰

    Monthly Budget

    Income, expenses, and category limits with spending-pace marks that turn amber when you're ahead of the month. Set every limit on one screen, file entries on the day they happened, and let bills settle themselves when the real charge arrives - a bill calendar shows what's due and paid, and a safe-to-spend number replaces guesswork.

  • 🏦

    Bank Connections

    Bring your own SimpleFIN or Teller credentials and let transactions import themselves into any account, checking to 401k - nothing enters your budget until you approve it in the Review Inbox, which learns your rules, flags unusual charges, and spots subscriptions with no bill on file. If a bank needs a fresh login, the app says so and re-imports the gap when it's back. No aggregator, no server in between.

  • 🧭

    The Bridge

    Your net worth dashboard: accounts, history, rise/drop tracking over a day, week, month, or quarter, and a "This month" card with your spending pace and latest entries. Add live stock and crypto holdings (opt-in), link your emergency fund to real savings accounts, see what each account earns at its APY, and run Purchase Plans that add up - snowball-style ordering, one monthly amount across every plan, and a chart of them all.

  • 📈

    Charts & Calculators

    Compound interest projections that compare a lump sum with monthly contributions, loan and mortgage amortization, refinance break-even, a US take-home pay estimator that never phones home, currency exchange, "What if I stopped spending on…", a Plan-a-Purchase tool with cost per use and save-vs-finance, and a Subscription Detective. Try them all in your browser.

  • 🛶

    Build Your Ark

    A milestone journey for your finances: lay the keel, patch the hull, stock the galley. Quick-start budget templates get you moving, and your ark takes shape as your money does.

  • 🎓

    Captain's Course

    All 24 lessons across five chapters - from good vs. bad debt to index funds, insurance, and estate basics - plus a Ship's Log of achievements with live progress rings to earn along the way.

  • 🧾

    Receipts, Business & People

    Snap encrypted receipt photos onto any entry, tag expenses to a business for a tax-time report and CSV, mark paychecks W-2 or 1099, and split spending across the people in your household - then Settle Up shows who owes what each month.

  • 🤝

    Partner Sync

    Budget together. Pair with your partner's device and sync encrypted, directly over your own Wi-Fi - no server in the middle. Mark an entry Private and it stays on your phone only.

  • 🔐

    App Lock & Privacy

    Everything is stored encrypted on your device. Add a PIN gate on launch, block screenshots with Privacy Mode, and skip the sign-up entirely - no email, no account, no analytics.

  • 📤

    Backups & Spreadsheets

    Scheduled local auto-backups, tamper-evident encrypted exports, and a full Excel workbook with live formulas that round-trips back in. Your data is yours - take it anywhere.

  • 📱

    Widget & Search

    Log an expense in one tap from a home-screen Quick Entry widget on iOS and Android, and find anything - debts, payments, entries - with global search and advanced filters.

QUESTIONS FROM THE DOCK

Frequently asked

Is BudgetArk really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no paid tier, no ads, and no feature behind a paywall. An optional Tip Jar exists for people who want to say thanks, and it unlocks nothing because everything is already included. The calculators are free on this site too, no app required.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Completely. BudgetArk is offline-first, and every core feature works with your phone in airplane mode. The optional online features - bank connections, live investment prices, currency rates, partner sync, and the Tip Jar - stay off until you turn them on, and each one explains exactly what it sends before it runs.

How can it connect to my bank if there is no BudgetArk server?

You bring your own SimpleFIN or Teller credentials, and your phone talks to that provider directly over HTTPS. BudgetArk runs no aggregator and never sits in between. Imported transactions wait in a Review Inbox on your device, and nothing enters your budget until you approve it.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only, encrypted with AES-256 and authenticated with HMAC-SHA256. The key lives in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore and never leaves the phone. There is no account to create and no BudgetArk server that could hold a copy.

Can I export my data?

Yes, in several formats: encrypted JSON backups, a full Excel workbook with live formulas that imports back in, CSV exports for tax reports and amortization schedules, and an archive of your receipt photos. Scheduled local auto-backups run on the device too.

Can I work with a financial advisor using BudgetArk?

Yes, on your terms. There is no advisor portal or shared account - nothing leaves your phone unless you export it - but the exports are built for exactly this. Hand your advisor the Excel workbook, which lays out your budget, debts, accounts, and holdings with live formulas they can work in, or a CSV of the business report or a loan's amortization schedule. The Bridge gives them your net worth and its history at a glance, and the Debt Tracker shows every balance, rate, and payoff plan in one place. When the meeting is over, nothing is left on anyone's server.

What is the Captain's Course?

A built-in money course: 24 short lessons, a few minutes each, in five chapters that follow the Build Your Ark order - Setting Sail (budgeting basics), Patching the Hull (debt: good vs. bad debt, how interest works, snowball vs. avalanche, refinancing), Stocking the Galley (emergency funds, high-yield savings, sinking funds), Catching Wind (compounding, index funds, 401(k)s, IRAs and Roth, asset allocation), and Charting Far Waters (net worth, buy vs. rent, insurance, estate basics). It lives on the Charts tab, works offline, is free like everything else, and your progress earns Ship's Log badges and rides along in backups. It is general education from the app's developer, not personalised advice.

Can I budget together with a partner?

Yes. Partner Sync pairs two phones directly over your own Wi-Fi with end-to-end encryption and no cloud relay. Mark any entry Private and it stays on your phone only.

When is the Android version coming?

Android is in closed testing on Google Play and anyone can join: join the tester group, opt in, and install - all with the same Google account you use on the Play Store. The public launch will be announced on the news page.
